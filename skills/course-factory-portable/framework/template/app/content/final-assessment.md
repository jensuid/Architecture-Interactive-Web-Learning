## How this works

The final quiz lives in **Module 3** (quiz ids `final-*`) — the template ships
it there to demonstrate the `FINAL_QUIZ_HOST` dual-recording mechanism. If you
prefer the final quiz on this page instead, move it here and set
`FINAL_QUIZ_HOST = null` in `shell.js`.
